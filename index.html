COMMON QUESTIONS

Know before you go.

How do I book a hunt or trip?

Start by sending an inquiry with your name, trip type, preferred dates, and group size. Glengarry Outfitters can then reply with details, availability, and next steps.

Can I ask about group trips?

Yes. Include your group size and any trip preferences in the inquiry form so the options can be discussed directly.

Do you offer hunting and fishing?

Yes. Ask about hunting opportunities or guided fishing trips. Specific offerings and availability are confirmed directly.

Where do I inquire?

Use the inquiry button to prepare your message, then send it through our Instagram account.
